My Projects

A few things I've built or worked on lately:

1. AI Newsletter (OneWorldGreaterTogether)

A weekly email newsletter about AI updates and tools for students and teachers. I put together a starter pack with prompt lists and cheat sheets to help classmates test out AI on their own.

2. NotebookLM-Style Study Helper

A tool concept that condenses long textbook chapters into straightforward study notes. Built it so reviewing for math and science exams takes less time.

3. Cybersecurity PSA: "SPOT SCAMS"

An awareness project about social engineering. It breaks down how phishing and fake urgent messages trick people, plus quick checks to spot them before clicking.

4. School Marketplace Concept

A mock platform for high school students to swap textbooks, pass down calculators, and trade class supplies.
