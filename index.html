My Projects

Here are some of the projects and concepts I have worked on:

1. AI Newsletter (OneWorldGreaterTogether)

A weekly AI newsletter designed for students, parents, and teachers. It shares the latest AI news, useful tools, and practical guides. I also created a free starter kit with 100+ prompts, cheat sheets, and guides to help beginners get into AI.

2. NotebookLM-Style Study Helper

A study assistant concept that turns heavy textbook chapters into concise, clear, and easy-to-understand study notes. Built to make learning subjects like math and science much faster and clearer.

3. Cybersecurity PSA: "SPOT SCAMS"

An educational project focused on social engineering awareness. It teaches everyday internet users how scammers use fear, urgency, and false trust to trick people, and how to spot suspicious messages quickly.

4. School Marketplace Concept

A student-focused platform idea to help high schoolers share, exchange, and find school resources, study materials, and everyday student items easily.
